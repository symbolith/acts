# Clean-context writing discipline

Shared by the write-prose family: write-prose, write-gloss, and every micro-genre skill to come.

- The orchestrator never writes, rephrases, or tidies text. Writers draft in fresh contexts, verifiers check in fresh contexts, and both are relayed verbatim.
- Ground from disk at the start of every round; files change mid-session, and conversation memory of a file is contamination.
- Candidates are shown labelled, verbatim, without ranking.
- A rejection and its reason carry verbatim into every later prompt under `Rejected`.
- Apply with `Edit` only, after an explicit yes; never write into the target before acceptance.
- A recurring micro-genre (a remark, a heading, a paragraph form) gets its own skill before its first production run: research the traditions, map the design space, put the design questions to the user, test on real cases (the claude-code:create-skill process, as write-gloss went through it). Until that skill exists, the general pipeline handles the genre.

Read the provider's page of the accommodation and every page it links for renting.
Write an Accommodation note, and an Organization note for the provider unless one exists.
Take rents, fees, sizes and terms as the page states them and leave out what it does not state.
Write the body in German, as bullet points of a few words each, and link every page, form and address where the text names it.
Keep only what decides whether and how to move in: rent and fees, who may move in, minimum term, common areas, travel time, free rooms and how to ask for one.
